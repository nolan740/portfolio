01 / Business Problem

Structuring Fragmented Information into a Predictable Research Cadence

Personal investment research requires repetitive checks across positions, market quotes, capital flows, technical indicators, filings, and financial news. Fragmented data sources and the need to maintain consistent position states and evaluation criteria across consecutive trading sessions create significant cognitive overhead.

Objective

Establish a repeatable, automated workflow for intelligence ingestion, risk rules enforcement, candidate ranking, and structured report synthesis.

Human Boundary

The system provides decision support and synthesized research. Trade validation and final execution remain strictly manual; execution confirmations feed back into the system to calibrate position states for subsequent sessions.

02 / Workflow Design

From Position Inputs to Execution Feedback

  1. Trigger & IngestionRuns pre-market and pre-close sessions aligned with A-share trading days; loads current holdings and risk control rules.
  2. Data & Indicator RetrievalCalls quote APIs, capital flows, technical indicators, and corporate disclosures, switching to redundant feeds during anomalies.
  3. Comprehensive Synthesis & Rule ScreeningSynthesizes holdings, risk limits, and candidate criteria to produce structured investment hypotheses.
  4. Structured ReportingGenerates Markdown reports documenting data timestamps, top priorities, trigger thresholds, and risk factors.
  5. Human Decision MakingThe user reviews reports on PC / WeChat and determines actual order executions.
  6. Feedback & State SynchronizationLogs trade execution confirmations to update position states, priming the baseline for the next research session.

Runtime Environment: Codex + Heartbeat. Data Tooling: East Money Miaoxiang mx-data, mx-search, mx-xuangu, and fallback quote APIs. Scheduled sessions: 09:45 AM and 02:30 PM on trading days.

03 / Key Decisions

Ensuring Every Output Is Auditable, Recoverable, and Cohesive

Explicit Data Timestamps

Reports log generation time, session identifier, and underlying data timestamps standardized to Beijing Time (UTC+8) to eliminate scheduling skew.

Standardized Report Structure

Structured into market overviews, stock-by-stock analyses, trigger conditions, and 1–3 prioritized actions, reducing reading and comparison overhead.

State Driven by Execution Feedback

Sequences subsequent sessions based on manually verified fills, mitigating drift in share quantities and cost baselines.

Delivery Validation

Catches timeouts, empty payloads, ungenerated reports, and stale reads through automated retries, fallback sources, and write validation.

07 / Contributions & Limitations

Refining Long-Running Workflows Through Empirical Feedback

My Contributions

  • Collaborated with Codex to iteratively refine data ingestion, fallback sources, retry policies, file overwrites, and state synchronization logic.

Current Limitations
