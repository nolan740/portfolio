01 / Business Problem

Establishing a Repeatable Screening Pipeline Across Job Platforms

Job seekers frequently deal with fragmented searches across multiple platforms, opening full job descriptions individually, cross-referencing resumes, and tracking links and review statuses manually. Information dispersion, duplicate listings, and truncated details substantially drive up manual screening overhead.

Objective

Streamline job discovery, incremental change detection, full JD validation, match grading, and record indexing into a unified automated pipeline.

Human Boundary

The workflow automates discovery, filtering, and record keeping. Position evaluation, direct outreach, actual submission, and final decisions remain strictly manual.

02 / Workflow Design

From Candidate Persona to an Actionable Job Pool

  1. Trigger & ProfilingRuns at 09:30 AM every business day; loads target roles, geographic preferences, and qualification rules.
  2. Multi-Source Discovery & DeduplicationExecutes jobfindsme, detecting incremental opportunities via job fingerprints and historical records.
  3. Hard-Constraint FilteringEnforces strict criteria including industry exclusions, employment types, compensation benchmarks, and commute constraints.
  4. Full JD VerificationNavigates to original posting pages via web-access / Chrome CDP to verify responsibilities, requirements, and active URLs.
  5. Match Grading & IngestionApplies a 100-point rubric to generate Tier A/B/C classifications, highlighting key alignments and risk factors.
  6. Manual Review & RecoveryLogs listings into the review table; runs a 10:30 AM failure sweep to rerun and recover incomplete tasks.

Tool Responsibilities: job-radar-cn governs candidate personas and scoring; jobfindsme handles discovery and state tracking; web-access / Chrome CDP verifies original pages; Excel and SQLite persist candidate pools and run states.

03 / Key Decisions

Prioritizing Verifiability Over Broad Coverage

Complete JD as Ingestion Gate

Listings lacking complete job descriptions or valid primary links are withheld from ingestion with explicit error reasons. Earlier records lacking links are flagged as legacy items for audit.

Tiered Rules and Scoring

Targeting the Shanghai market, prioritizing AI Product roles with Enterprise Internal Products as secondary; hard exclusions filter first, followed by a 100-point rubric for tier classification.

Documenting Alignment Points and Risks

Outputs concrete qualification alignments and conditions requiring verification, providing substantive rationale for human review rather than an opaque score.

Precise Definition of 'New'

'New' indicates first-time discovery within this pipeline; platform publication timestamps are independently audited. Cross-platform fuzzy deduplication and commute modeling remain active iterations.

06 / Fault Recovery

Converting Failures into Actionable, Recoverable States

Handling JD Extraction Failures

When job detail retrieval experienced anomalies, ingestion was automatically paused; once connectivity was restored, verification was re-executed, successfully adding 1 Tier A listing.

Explicit Reporting & Catch-up Runs

Authentication drops, bridging errors, timeouts, or spreadsheet write failures trigger explicit alerts; automated retries combined with a 10:30 AM failure sweep mitigate missed runs.
